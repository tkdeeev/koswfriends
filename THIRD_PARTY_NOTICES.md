## Keeping notices with a release

The `public/licenses` directory is copied into the production image, with its files exposed under `/licenses/`. Keep it with browser bundles and fonts. Do not strip the dependencies' own license and notice files when building an image. For a new dependency version, check its actual packaged license, copyright and any `NOTICE` file, refresh copied notices, and recheck platform-specific binaries. Do not infer license obligations solely from an npm package's short license identifier.
